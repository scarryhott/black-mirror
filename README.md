# Black Mirror

This repository contains the paper **Black Mirror: Translational Truth, Natural Choice, and the Architecture of Relational Invariance**.

The paper develops Black Mirror as a metaphysical and systems-design problem: a relative representation becomes dangerous when it is mistaken for the whole relation that generated it. The proposed alternative is **translational truth**, where truth equality is defined through admissible translation, preservation, and return among relative presentations.

## Main paper

- [BLACK_MIRROR_TRANSLATIONAL_TRUTH.md](BLACK_MIRROR_TRANSLATIONAL_TRUTH.md)

## Core themes

- natural choice as relational form rather than isolated optimization
- translational truth and relative depth of completion
- Gödel, Turing, and Tarski as precise local limits rather than complete theories of truth
- non-finitary continuous closure when axiom and geometry are unified
- four-phase partition waves and unitary-curvature return
- informational likeness and tokenized verification of mutual intent
- probability, information, and calculus chain rules as local/global reversal structures
- local representation with global action through agentic second brains
- potential compression when technological power outruns metaphysical and socioeconomic integration
- Black Mirror as representational capture, and translation as the alternative

The paper explicitly distinguishes formal mathematical results from metaphysical interpretation and speculative physical claims.
